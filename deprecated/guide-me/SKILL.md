# Guide me

Take a developer who cannot yet state what they want to an approved goal and into the right
planning workflow. Guide-me writes no product code and no implementation issues.

## Understand

Run the questioning loop in [understand-work](../understand-work/SKILL.md) until the goal stops
moving or the developer says it is done.

## Explain

Run the plain-language gate in [explain-work](../explain-work/SKILL.md) on the settled
understanding. A challenge reopens understand-work at the misunderstood point. Do not continue
without an approved summary.

## Continue

The approved summary decides the route:

- several independent decision threads → continue into `chart-work`;
- one bounded idea with material product choices left → continue into `shape-work`;
- something already small and clear → hand the developer a ready-to-send direct request instead of
  a workflow.

The developer's approval at the gate is the opt-in for this continuation. Announce the route, then
proceed with the approved summary and the settled understanding as the handoff. Place the approved
summary as a `## TLDR` at the top of the artifact the next workflow creates, so the map or shape
opens with what the work solves in plain language. Ruled-out directions and open questions travel
with the handoff; nothing already answered is asked again.
