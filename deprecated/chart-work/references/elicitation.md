# Elicitation and evidence

Ask for the smallest evidence that can settle the active ticket.

## Developer choice

Follow the questioning loop in [understand-work](../../understand-work/SKILL.md). When words are
insufficient, use a prototype.

## Research

Use repository evidence, primary sources, or direct measurements. Cite the decisive source or
observation and state uncertainty that could change the decision.

## Prototype

Follow [prototypes.md](prototypes.md). Separate observed behavior from the developer's judgment.

## Prerequisite task

Do only the prerequisite needed to make the decision stateable. Record the result and the question
it unlocked.

Do not treat silence, momentum, or approval of a larger bundle as an answer to a specific product
choice.
