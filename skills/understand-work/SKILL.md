# Understand work

Find the need behind the stated wish. The deliverable is a settled understanding — what the
developer wants, why now, and what was ruled out — not a plan or a solution.

## Questioning loop

Resolve everything the repository, project policy, and conversation already answer before asking
anything. Then, for each open point that materially changes the goal:

- Present the concrete tension with materially different alternatives.
- Recommend one and name its consequence.
- Ask one load-bearing question at a time and wait for the answer.
- Record the choice, the reason, and every ruled-out direction with its reason.

Dig beneath the stated wish: what becomes possible when this exists, who feels the difference, and
why it matters now. Do not treat silence, momentum, or approval of a larger bundle as an answer to
a specific question. Do not propose a technical solution while the goal is still moving.

## Exit

Questioning ends when the developer says it is done or when further answers stop changing the goal.
Deliver the settled understanding: the goal, its motivation, the choices made with their reasons,
the ruled-out directions, and the questions that remain open. Under guide-me this feeds the
plain-language gate in explain-work; standalone, report it directly.
