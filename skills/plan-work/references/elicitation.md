# Planning evidence

Use the smallest evidence that can settle the active planning question.

## Developer choice

Follow the questioning loop in [understand-work](../../understand-work/SKILL.md). Present materially
different options, recommend one, name the consequence, and ask one load-bearing question at a
time. The developer supplies preference evidence; the agent does not invent it.

## Research

Use repository evidence, primary sources, or direct measurements. Cite the decisive source or
observation and state uncertainty that could change the decision.

## Prototype

Follow [prototypes.md](prototypes.md). Keep observed behavior separate from the developer's
judgment and stop when the question is settled or the bounded effort budget is exhausted.

## Prerequisite task

Do only the prerequisite needed to make the decision stateable. Record the result and the question
it unlocked. Do not turn a newly discovered technical detail into a blocking side issue when it is
within the current mission and mandate.

Silence, momentum, and approval of a larger bundle are never an answer to a specific product
choice.
