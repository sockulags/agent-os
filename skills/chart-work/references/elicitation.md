# Elicitation and evidence

Read this before resolving any decision ticket. Ask for the smallest evidence that can settle the
ticket; do not turn the session into a general requirements interview.

## Grilling

1. Ask what hurts, fails, or feels wrong in the current experience.
2. Present two or three materially different alternatives. Name real reference products only when
   the comparison is accurate enough to make the trade-off clearer.
3. Recommend one alternative and state its main consequence.
4. Ask the user to react to the alternatives, one load-bearing decision at a time.
5. Capture why the user chose, not only which option they chose.

Silence, momentum, approval of the map, or approval of a bundle is not an answer to an individual
ticket. When the user cannot judge the difference in words, convert the ticket to `prototype`.

## Prototype

Build the smallest disposable comparison that makes the contested behavior observable. Offer the
alternatives side by side or behind one obvious toggle, verify that the artifact actually runs, and
ask the user to perform the shortest meaningful task. Record the artifact, observation, and user's
judgment; the prototype is evidence, not the destination deliverable.

## Research

Answer factual questions with primary sources, repository evidence, or direct measurements. Record
source links, commands, relevant outputs, and uncertainty. A plausible explanation without an
inspectable source or measurement does not close the ticket.

## Task

Perform or hand off only the prerequisite action required to make a later decision stateable. Record
what changed, the receipt that proves it, and the decision it unlocked. A task may be AFK when it
does not require human authority; otherwise give the human a precise checklist and wait.

## Evidence threshold

Evidence must match the decision:

- user preference: explicit answer plus rationale or observed reaction;
- external or codebase fact: cited source, code trace, or reproducible measurement;
- experiential choice: verified prototype plus observation;
- prerequisite: completion receipt plus the newly unlocked question.

Record unresolved uncertainty under Open risks rather than smoothing it into the decision.
