# Frontend mockups

Use a mockup only when seeing the behavior will improve a decision.

## Grounding

Inspect the project's tokens, components, and nearby views. Reuse established patterns. When the
system lacks a needed pattern, label the proposal as new instead of presenting it as existing.

## Fidelity scaling

- **Sketch or wireframe:** enough to compare structure, states, or flow.
- **Renderable proposal:** useful when visual hierarchy or interaction behavior cannot be judged
  from a sketch.

Build only the fidelity needed for the open decision.

## Evidence

Show the states and viewport sizes relevant to the decision, including keyboard or failure behavior
when those are in scope. Keep the artifact inspectable and capture the developer's decision and the
reason behind it. Implementation authority comes from the later implementation request, not from a
separate mockup approval state.
